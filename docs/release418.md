# Internal 418 · 1.0.366

## Included
- Stable main opinions with multiple visible reactions. Main opinions may be queued during another opinion and follow its response window. Reactions add one pressure point; silence and stance-change controls are available.
- Five-minute whole-meeting countdown; all surviving human players can skip together, advancing after two seconds. NPCs do not vote to skip.
- New games use two daytime activity periods, meeting/vote, private Mafia night-target selection, then morning discovery. Daytime kills are disabled in this cycle. Existing games retain their day/night cycle.
- Evidence shown as sentences. Presence observations alone do not increase pressure as proof. Specific time/place claims can be compared with firsthand surveys or the named partner’s own record. Intuition is explicitly uncertain, not role detection.
- Game abilities page between former pages 9 and 10: deception, intuition, stealth, reasoning, observation, composure. Saved/shared with the character and used in observations/impressions.
- Flow layout for expanding meeting speech and multiple reactions, no bottom scene-record block. Evidence at upper left, muted contextual memory; private evidence choices inline.
- Single-bed visual dimensions halved from 417; duplicate catalog entry hidden while existing furniture persists. Sleeping status removed from the body anchor; pillow/quilt layers retained.
- Need recovery projected continuously during activity, without double credit on save. Toilet recovery approximately one minute and its task duration one minute.
- Furniture Other activities now constrained to that furniture. The complete activity catalog remains accessible from character commands.

## Validation
- 30 updated meeting games, 30 night-cycle games, 30 legacy games finish.
- Multiple reactions, queued main opinion, reaction pressure, silence/stance, specific contradiction, private evidence authorization, human-only skip +2s, no daytime kill, private night target and morning discovery.
- Chrome/WebKit KO/EN/JA small-screen meeting controls, long dialogue geometry, private night selection.
- Chrome/WebKit contextual furniture filtering, complete self activity catalog, actual hug, abilities saved and reloaded/shared.
- Single-bed front/right/left geometry and base/person/quilt layers; needs continuous recovery/no double credit; seating regressions.
- Native module closure and packaged asset validation.

New/changed UI text: English 100%, Japanese 100%. Whole application coverage not measured. Real multi-device latency and gameplay balance still require internal playtesting; automated completion is not a balance claim.
